# ADR-NNNN: Decision title

Template only; copy to an allocated filename. Read the [policy](policy.md).
Do not list a copied proposal as accepted. On explicit acceptance, set `status`
to `accepted`, fill the actual `date`, record the approval reference and update
the index. Remove authoring instructions before submission.

## Decision

State the proposed or accepted rule, its exact scope and preserved invariants.
Identify what remains undecided. Do not describe a target as implemented.

## Constraints / Do NOT

State scoped prohibitions and their short reasons, including applicable lessons
from replaced decisions. Do not turn every unselected alternative into a global
ban. Write `None identified` when accurate.

## Context & Reason

Explain the problem, constraints, triage result and why this choice is warranted.
Summarize decisive alternatives briefly; do not embed rejected implementations.
For a replacement, map changed and preserved clauses to their successor.
For an additive refinement, explain why the earlier decision remains valid.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, accepted costs, risks, and follow-up work. Separate decision
acceptance, implementation plans and actually executed verification. Acceptance
examples are future requirements until execution evidence is recorded.

## References

Record approval/discussion references, relevant current design documents and
stack dependencies. Keep the essential rationale in this file. Use `replaces`
for retired-file identifiers rather than live links to deleted files; include a
known pre-retirement commit when useful. Do not invent an approval or a SHA.
